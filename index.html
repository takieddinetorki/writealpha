About the book

For the teacher who stares at a stack of essays on a Friday afternoon and wonders if there is a better way: there is.

This is a practical, principled guide for English language teachers navigating a world where artificial intelligence has arrived in the classroom, whether they invited it or not. Every technique flows from one core belief: protect student thinking, preserve teacher judgment, and never automate what deserves human attention.

  • 21 chapters covering every major essay type, the CLEAR prompting framework, and a five-agent AI feedback system
  • The single highest-leverage practice in the book: using an AI agent to check student writing and deliver targeted feedback in minutes, not hours
  • Built for real Gulf EFL classrooms - large class sizes, mixed proficiency levels, and tools that change faster than the training
  • A principled framework for professional decisions about AI that will still work when today's tools are gone

"AI is both tool and tutor. But the teacher remains irreplaceable."
