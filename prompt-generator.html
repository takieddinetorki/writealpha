Writing Prompt Generator

Choose your class level, writing type, and topic area. Get a ready-to-use student prompt - plus the CLEAR framework version to use with any AI tool.

How to use this tool

1
Students write first - give them the student prompt. They attempt the task independently before any AI assistance.
2
AI as scaffold - once they have a first attempt, they can use the CLEAR prompt with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for vocabulary, structure ideas, or feedback questions.
3
Log every AI interaction - students complete the AI Collaboration Log for any AI they use. Download it from the Templates page.
4
The CLEAR rule - the CLEAR prompt tells AI what NOT to do. AI suggests vocabulary or ideas; students write. If AI writes the paragraph, the task is over.
