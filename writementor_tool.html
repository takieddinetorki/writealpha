Welcome to

WriteMentor

for students and teachers

This is where you paste your writing and get instant, teacher-like feedback. Mistakes are underlined in different colors by category — grammar, vocabulary, sentence structure, word order, prepositions, punctuation, capital letters — each with a plain-English hint. No grammar jargon. Free to use, open to everyone — no sign-up required.

Your name or ID (optional)

This is just so your teacher can tell sessions apart. It isn't verified, so use whatever name you like.

How to use it
  1. Step 1. Paste your writing, or write it directly in the box provided — up to 1,500 words. Shorter texts get checked faster, especially on a slower internet connection — a paragraph or two works best if speed matters more than length.
  2. Step 2. Click "Check my writing." WriteMentor underlines every mistake in the color of its category.
  3. Step 3. Hover over (or tap) any underlined mistake to see a hint — the answer isn't shown yet.
  4. Step 4. Think about the mistake and correct it yourself, directly on the page.
  5. Step 5. Click "Check my correction" to see your progress — you can now also reveal the fully corrected version to compare.
